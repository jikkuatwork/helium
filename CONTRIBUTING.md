# Contributing to Helium

Thank you for helping. Issues and pull requests are welcome on GitHub.

## Contributor License Agreement

Every contributor signs a CLA, so the rights in each contribution are settled
up front: you keep the copyright in your work, and the Maintainer gets a
permanent, irrevocable license to use, relicense and transfer it.

**To sign:** open your pull request, read [CLA.md](CLA.md), then post this
exact comment on the pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The CLA bot records your signature once, and it covers your later pull
requests too. If the check does not update, comment `recheck`.

**Corporate contributors:** if your employer owns what you write, an
authorised signatory signs [CLA-CORPORATE.md](CLA-CORPORATE.md) and lists you
on its Schedule A. You still sign the individual CLA on your pull request.

## Your Own Work

Each commit must be your own work, or work you have the right to submit under
the CLA. If any part comes from someone else, say so in the pull request and
give its source and license (see section 8 of the CLA). In the spirit of the
Developer Certificate of Origin, you may add a `Signed-off-by:` line
(`git commit -s`) to record this.
